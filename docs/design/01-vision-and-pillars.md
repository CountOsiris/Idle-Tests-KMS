# 01 · Vision and pillars

Back to the [Masterplan](../../Masterplan.md).

## What it should feel like

You open the game after a night away. Your Warden fell eleven times, banked gold and
experience, gained four levels and pushed two floors deeper. The blacksmith can turn
your Iron Spiked Shield +7 into +9. A milestone perk is waiting. You spend a minute
deciding, then watch a fight for a while because a boss is close and you want to see
whether the new shield holds. Then you close the tab.

Once a week something big happens: a wall falls, a breakthrough lands, or a trophy
from another class's tower changes how a build plays. That is the rhythm: a
satisfying minute every visit, and a reason to come back for the big moments.

## How I picture the game (as it is built today)

A dark, warm fantasy page in parchment gold on near-black, set in Cinzel and EB Garamond.

- **The stage.** Left column. Your class on the left, the monster on the right, health
  bars, floating damage numbers, a lunge and a shake on every hit. The tower's colour
  tints the scene. Bosses glow; a boss kill drops a "Victory" banner.
- **Under the stage.** The floor and four room pips, what the monster is weak to and
  resists, its armor and ward, then "what to do next" (up to three lines) and the
  boons this run.
- **The tabs.** Right column. Character, Inventory, Skills, Town, Towers, Milestones,
  Ascension, Settings. Each is a stack of cards with rows of "name, note, button".
- **The pulse.** One step per second. Small toasts for levels, floors, relics, boons.
  The log on the left tells the story.

It reads like a ledger beside a small theatre. The theatre is where feel comes from
and is the part most worth investing in (see [UI, UX and feel](09-ui-ux-and-feel.md)).

## Pillars

1. **Numbers go big, forever.** No top levels. Past a cap, overflow becomes something
   else (crit chance past 100% becomes crit damage).
2. **Death is the goal, never a risk.** No death tax, no "bank early" button.
3. **Seven ways to fight.** Each class has distinct mechanics, each weapon a distinct
   build. No dead builds.
4. **Your tower, then theirs.** Home tower to grow; away towers as challenges for
   permanent unlocks.
5. **Steady drip, rare floods.** See the reward cadence in
   [Core loop and pacing](02-core-loop-and-pacing.md).
6. **Idle-first, active-rewarding.** Never a timer the player must beat. Active play
   may be faster or more interesting, never required.

## Reference games, and what we take from each

| Game | What it does well | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| **Echoes of Creation** (Echoforge, 2025) | Auto-battles you can watch and learn from; 40+ abilities; 120+ build-defining unique items; a shrine whose permanent blessings help every character; dungeons of rising difficulty; inspired by Diablo II and Path of Exile. | Combat worth watching, abilities, build-defining unlocks, account-wide blessings (our fame already is one). |
| **Cell to Singularity** | A tree of milestones that each reveal something new; a meta layer (Reality Engine) that speeds future runs. | Unlocking systems as milestones, not all at once. Players complain of "a straight up wall" after a few upgrades there: walls need a visible way through. |
| **Realm Grinder** | Factions that change the whole game; prestige based on the square root of earnings; later layers (reincarnation) that shrink numbers back down. | Classes as factions; a second prestige layer later. |
| **Antimatter Dimensions** | Layered prestige where each layer unlocks a new mechanic, not just a multiplier; challenges that reward permanent upgrades. | Challenges with rules, each layer opening a new system. |
| **Clicker Heroes** | Bosses as gates; "ancients" bought with prestige currency. | Boss gates; prestige shops with real choices. |
| **AdVenture Capitalist** (Pecorella) | Milestone multipliers at owned counts (x2 at 25, 50...) that make buying spike. | Rank milestones on skills (done) and the blacksmith (to do). |

## What this game is not

- Not a loot game. Enemies do not drop gear (decided October 2026). Build-defining
  items, if they come, are earned from challenges and bosses, not rolled.
- Not a clicker. Clicking never speeds a fight.
- Not a gacha. No paid randomness, no energy, no timers that punish leaving.
