# 12 · Open questions

Back to the [Masterplan](../../Masterplan.md).

Decisions that change the plan. When one is answered, record the answer in the file it
affects and strike it here.

1. **Abilities: idle or chosen in the moment?** The plan assumes abilities fire by
   themselves (you pick *which*, never *when*). Do you want any manual "press now"
   option for active players, or fully automatic?
2. **Challenge rewards: per class or account-wide?** Fame is now shared. Should a
   trophy or technique won by the Warden help every class, only the Warden, or the
   Warden fully and others at a share (like fame's catch-up)?
3. **Build-defining items.** Echoes of Creation leans on 120+ uniques. With loot drops
   ruled out, are *named weapons earned from challenges* the right replacement, or do
   you want something else (crafting from boss materials, for example)?
4. **Flat-to-percentage conversion.** Converting old flat rewards changes numbers on
   existing saves (ids stay, so nothing is lost). OK to do it in one update, with a
   note to players?
5. **How hard should away towers be?** Today: +100% health, +50% attack, resistance up
   to 60%. Should a challenge feel winnable in a day, a week, or be an endgame project?
6. **Second prestige layer.** Is a "Legend" layer after floor 100 the direction, or
   should endless breakthroughs stay the only late-game system for now?
7. **Phones.** How many of your players are on a phone? It decides how much effort goes
   into the mobile layout in Phase 1.
8. **The Elementalist's element.** It can be changed any time, unlike every other
   class's weapon (which waits for the next run). Keep that as its special trait?
