Relics

0 held

Relics are rare, and each one changes how the run plays. The boss of every 25th floor carries one, and now and then a rare monster does. They last until you fall, and the same relic can be held more than once. Very rarely, a relic boss you fight also carries the legendary relic of the weapon in your hand: it multiplies that weapon's damage and your health for the rest of the run.

Abilities

Slots 0

Special moves used on a cooldown, as well as your normal attack. Each one in a slot can be used by itself (Auto), only against bosses (Bosses only), or only when you press its button under the fight (Manual). An ability put in a slot waits out its cooldown before its first use; a new run readies them all. An ability gains ranks as it is used, and keeps them for good.

Most abilities deal "turns of your damage": what your normal turns have been doing lately, crits, bleeding and all. Yours: 0 a turn.

Skills

Skill points 0

Every level gives a skill point. Skills have no top level. Every 10 levels a skill gains a rank: the whole skill becomes 50% stronger, and its next levels cost 1 point more. They last until you ascend, and resetting them is free.

Towers

Every class has a home tower that suits it. Any other tower is a challenge: its monsters are stronger and it has a rule against challengers, but it pays more experience and holds six trophies, at floors 10, 20, 30, 50, 75 and 100. A trophy is a permanent reward for the class that wins it, and they grow: a percentage, then a technique borrowed from that tower's class, an ability, a relic you keep when you fall, and at floor 100 a new rule of your own. A new tower is entered after your next fall. Each row shows the next trophy to win; "All trophies" lists the rest.

Milestones

Reach a floor once to unlock its perks forever, even through ascensions. Pick one per milestone and change it whenever you like. A ★ keystone changes a rule of how you fight, and costs something. Keystone milestones (floors 51, 76 and 101) are optional: press the chosen one again to let it go.

Ascension

Fame 0

Every floor pays 1 fame the first time you reach it. Ascending starts this class again from level 1, and makes every floor pay again. From floor 15 up, every boss also pays fame each time you beat it, and deeper bosses pay more. Fame is shared by all your classes: whichever class earns it, what it buys makes every one of them stronger.

What is kept, and what is lost

There are three ways a class starts over. Falling costs almost nothing, ascending costs this climb, and becoming a legend (from floor 100) costs the tower.

Fame upgrades

Kept forever, and shared by all your classes. Every one is a straight multiplier, and every level multiplies again. They open up as you ascend. A class gets the full power of as many levels as it has ascended itself (this one: 0); levels beyond that work at 25% until it catches up.

Legend

Legend marks 0

A class that has reached floor 100 can become a legend. It starts again from floor 1 and level 1 with every milestone to earn again, and is paid legend marks: more for a deeper best floor, but four times as deep pays only twice as much. Marks are shared by all your classes.

Kept forever, for every class. None of these is a multiplier: each one opens something new.
